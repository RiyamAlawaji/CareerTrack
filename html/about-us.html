About the Project

CareerTrack is a website for university students and fresh graduates to organize their internship and job search. Instead of keeping applications in notes, messages and emails, the student records each application in one place, follows its status, remembers interview dates, and keeps the CV used for each application.

💡

Our goal is to make the job and internship application process simpler, more organized, and less stressful for students and graduates.
